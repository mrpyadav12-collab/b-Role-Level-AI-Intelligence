# Demo script

## 1. Introduction
Explain the business problem: understanding how AI will change workflows and reskilling needs across roles.

## 2. Architecture overview
Show the architecture: frontend -> FastAPI -> scoring logic -> AI interpretation -> SQLite data layer.

## 3. Dashboard
Open the dashboard and explain the seeded organisation and role inventory.

## 4. Existing role analysis
Choose a role such as Bank Teller or Mortgage Underwriter.
Show:
- activities
- AI exposure
- automation vs augmentation
- future responsibilities
- future skills
- evidence

## 5. Compare roles
Compare two roles such as Finance Analyst and Procurement Analyst, or the equivalent seeded roles in the app.

## 6. Highest impact roles
Show the top affected roles and explain why they score highly.

## 7. Surprise record test
Create a new role such as ESG Data Steward or Supply Chain Analyst.
Show the new role flow from input -> analysis -> persistence -> result.

## 8. Explainability
Highlight scoring factors and rationale from the analysis result.

## 9. Persistence
Refresh or restart the backend and confirm the role remains.

## 10. Close
Explain scale, future improvements, and why the design is robust.
